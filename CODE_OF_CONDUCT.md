# Code of conduct

This project is for people who build and use terminal programs. Take part in a
way that makes it easy for others to do the same.

## Expected

- Be direct and kind. Criticise code, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Keep to the topic of an issue or pull request, and say so when you change it.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's identity.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, or repeated off-topic posts.

## Scope

This applies in the repository: issues, pull requests, Discussions, and
reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove a comment, and may lock a thread or
block a person who breaks these rules. To report a problem, use a private
report on the Security tab (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and say it is a
conduct report. Reports are handled in private.
